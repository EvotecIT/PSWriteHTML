# Working agreements

Resolve shared procedures against `CODEX_HOME` (default `~/.codex`). Before adding or changing tests, apply [the test authoring gate](skills/test-audit/SKILL.md#authoring-gate) and [contract-focused proof design](skills/contract-focused-tests/SKILL.md). Deliberate test pruning follows [campaign mode](skills/test-audit/CAMPAIGN.md); its coverage evidence is local and never a CI or merge gate.
